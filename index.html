Case summary

Incident overview

On October 12, 18-year-old Ethan Miller arrived at a remote cabin for a quiet vacation. A man with a gun pursued him through the woods. The following night, a woman gained his trust, then restrained him. A supermarket cashier's warning and a timely police call helped save Ethan.

03 / Sequence of events

Reconstruction based on Ethan's account and the cashier's report.

Event timeline

Chronological reconstruction

DAY 1 — Arrival

Ethan arrived at the isolated cabin, unpacked, and prepared dinner. He had chosen the location because he wanted a quiet vacation.

DAY 1 — The pursuit

After hearing noises, Ethan went outside. He realized that a man was following him and saw a gun. He ran back and hid in a closet after the man had forced his way inside.

DAY 2 — The warning

At the supermarket, Michael Brooks warned Ethan that a killer was in the area. After Ethan had left, Michael noticed two suspicious people watching the cabin and called the police.

NIGHT 2 — The stranger

A frightened woman knocked on Ethan's door. He let her inside, believing that she needed help. After Ethan had fallen asleep, she tied him up.

NIGHT 2 — Rescue

Police sirens woke Ethan. Officers entered the cabin, arrested Sarah, and freed him. The cashier identified the couple as the suspicious people he had seen.

Final conclusion

Case disposition

The investigation concluded that Daniel Collins had pursued Ethan through the forest while Sarah Collins had gained his trust and restrained him inside the cabin. Sarah was arrested at the scene. Daniel was identified as her accomplice, but he remained at large when this report was filed.
